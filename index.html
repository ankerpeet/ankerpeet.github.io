Five things your website might be missing.

01

Accessibility

Find the things that get in people's way.

I check your key pages against WCAG 2.2 AA and try them with a keyboard and screen reader. That helps me find issues with forms, focus, contrast, and the smaller details that can make a site difficult to use.

02

Performance

Find out what's making the site feel slow.

I look at Core Web Vitals, JavaScript, images, and fonts to understand what's slowing the site down. Then I prioritize the fixes by how much they'll matter to your users.

03

Responsiveness

Check the sizes people actually use.

I test navigation, forms, and layouts on different screen sizes, including the awkward in-between ones. Small tap targets and layouts that break at certain widths are easy to miss if you only check a few standard sizes.

04

SEO

Help search engines understand your pages.

I use LibreCrawl to check the pages we agree on for technical SEO issues, then review the warnings before recommending changes. This is a selected-page review, not a whole-site crawl or a promise of higher rankings.

05

Security

Check the protections your site sends to browsers.

With your permission, I run a ZAP passive baseline and review findings such as missing security headers or cookie protections. There are no active attack tests, and a clean scan isn't proof that a site is secure.
