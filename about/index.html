About

Hi, I'm Anker Peet.

I'm a software engineer in Boise, Idaho with over 9 years of professional experience. Most recently I worked at Genesys, and before that at Healthwise (now WebMD Ignite).

The languages I'm most skilled in are JavaScript, TypeScript, and C#. My favorite part of being a software engineer has always been working on the frontend and building user interfaces that are functional and accessible.

Over the years I've noticed the same thing happen again and again. Teams move fast to ship features, and accessibility, performance, and responsive design slowly slip through the cracks. Nobody does it on purpose. Those problems are just easy to miss in day-to-day work.

That's why I started doing website audits. I'll go through your site, find what's actually getting in your users' way, and give your team a clear plan to fix it.

My approach

Senior engineering judgment, not scanner output.

I'll tell you what's broken, why it matters, and what your team should fix first.

Real users first

I test your site the way people actually use it: with a keyboard, a screen reader, a budget phone on a slow connection, or a browser zoomed in to 200%.

Prioritized by impact

Not every issue is a big deal. I rank everything by how much it affects your users and your business, so your team knows where to start.

Written for developers

I've been on the receiving end of plenty of bug reports. I write findings the way I'd want to get them: steps to reproduce, evidence, and code-level fixes.

Clear for decision-makers

Every report starts with a short summary that explains the risk, the effort, and the payoff in plain English.

Fixed scope, fixed price

We agree on the pages and the price before I start. No surprises.

Here after the report

I'll walk your team through everything I found, and I'm happy to stick around to help with fixes, re-testing, or monitoring.

Expertise

Where I can help

I focus on the parts of the frontend that have the biggest effect on how a site feels to use.

  • Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA, semantic HTML, ARIA patterns, keyboard and focus management, and assistive-technology testing.
  • Performance: Core Web Vitals, rendering performance, JavaScript cost, image and font optimization.
  • Responsive design: fluid layouts, modern CSS, touch-friendly interfaces, and cross-device testing.
  • Frontend architecture: component design, maintainable CSS, and practical engineering trade-offs.
