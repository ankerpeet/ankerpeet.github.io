About

Hi, I'm Anker Peet.

I'm a software engineer in Boise, Idaho. I've been doing this professionally for over 9 years, most recently at Genesys and before that at Healthwise (now WebMD Ignite).

I work mostly with JavaScript, TypeScript, and C#. I've always been drawn to frontend work because it's where the details of an interface become something people use.

I've also seen how easy it is for accessibility, performance, and responsive design to get missed when teams are busy shipping features. Usually nobody intends to leave those things behind; they just don't always show up in the day-to-day work.

That got me interested in website audits. I look at the site as people experience it, then share what I found and where I think the team should start.

I now also offer technical SEO reviews and passive security baselines. These check another part of website health: how search engines understand a page, and which browser protections the site provides. I review the findings rather than treating a scanner's result as the whole answer.

My approach

I look beyond what the scanner can tell us.

I'll explain what I found, why it matters, and what I think is worth fixing first.

Real users first

I try your site with a keyboard and screen reader, on a slower phone, and zoomed in. Those are a few ways people use the web that are easy to overlook when testing on a laptop.

Prioritized by impact

Not every issue has the same effect. I sort findings by how they affect people and the tasks they're trying to complete, so your team has a place to start.

Written for developers

I've received plenty of bug reports myself, so I try to make mine useful: what happened, how to reproduce it, and what might fix it.

Clear for decision-makers

The report starts with a short summary of the main findings and why they matter, without assuming everyone reading it works in code.

Fixed scope, fixed price

We'll agree on what I'm reviewing and what it will cost before I start.

Here after the report

I'll walk your team through what I found. If it would help, I can also work with you on fixes, re-testing, or monitoring.

Expertise

Where I can help

I focus on the parts of the frontend that shape how a site works for the people using it.

  • Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA, semantic HTML, ARIA patterns, keyboard and focus management, and assistive-technology testing.
  • Performance: Core Web Vitals, rendering performance, JavaScript cost, image and font optimization.
  • Responsive design: fluid layouts, modern CSS, touch-friendly interfaces, and cross-device testing.
  • Technical SEO: selected-page LibreCrawl checks with reviewed findings and practical recommendations.
  • Security baseline: authorized ZAP passive checks, with reviewed alerts and clear limits on what was tested.
  • Frontend architecture: component design, maintainable CSS, and practical engineering trade-offs.
