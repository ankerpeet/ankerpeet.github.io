About

Hi, I'm Anker Peet.

I'm a Boise-based software engineer with 9+ years of professional experience building production web applications, most recently at Genesys and Healthwise (now WebMD Ignite).

I work mainly in JavaScript, TypeScript, and C#, and my favorite part of the job has always been the frontend: building user interfaces that are functional, fast, and accessible. I've built software used by more than 11,000 customers across 100+ countries, and healthcare content delivered to patients billions of times.

Over that time I've seen the same pattern again and again: teams ship features fast, and accessibility, performance, and responsive polish quietly slip. Nobody decides to exclude users or frustrate them with a slow page; it happens because those concerns are hard to see in day-to-day work.

That's why I started offering website health audits. I bring a senior engineer's eye to your site, find what's actually getting in your users' way, and translate it into recommendations your team can ship.

My approach

Senior engineering judgment, not scanner output.

I translate frontend problems into practical recommendations: what is broken, why it matters, and what your team should fix first.

Real users first

I test the way people actually use your site: with a keyboard, a screen reader, a mid-range phone on a patchy connection, or a browser zoomed to 200%.

Prioritized by impact

Not every issue is equal. Findings are ranked by how much they affect users and the business, so your team knows exactly where to start.

Written for developers

As an engineer, I write findings the way I'd want to receive them: reproduction steps, evidence, and code-level recommendations.

Clear for decision-makers

Every report includes an executive summary that explains risk, effort, and payoff in plain language.

Fixed scope, fixed price

We agree on pages, journeys, and price before work begins. No surprises, no open-ended billing.

Here after the report

A personal walkthrough is always included, and I can stay on to help implement fixes, re-test, or monitor.

Expertise

Where I can help

My work focuses on the parts of the frontend that most directly shape how a site feels to use.

  • Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA, semantic HTML, ARIA patterns, keyboard and focus management, and assistive-technology testing.
  • Performance: Core Web Vitals, rendering performance, JavaScript cost, image and font optimization.
  • Responsive design: fluid layouts, modern CSS, touch-friendly interfaces, and cross-device testing.
  • Frontend architecture: component design, maintainable CSS, and practical engineering trade-offs.
